Privacy Policy

App: Hori Hori Tank Battle (HoriTan)
Effective date: October 12, 2026

This policy explains how AltVizn ("we") handles information in the mobile game "Hori Hori Tank Battle" (the "App").

1. Information we handle

The App has no sign-up. We never ask for your name, email address, phone number or postal address. When you use online features, the following information is handled:

InformationPurpose
An anonymous player ID (created automatically by the App)Connecting online matches; recording rankings
Your player name (chosen by you)Shown to opponents and in the ranking
Your self-made icon designShown to opponents and in the ranking
Rating, rank and match resultsCalculating and showing the ranking
In-match actions and network data such as IP addressRelaying online matches (not stored by us)

Your player name and icon are visible to other players. Please do not use names or images that identify you personally.

Reward progress, settings and your icons are stored on your device.

2. Advertising

The App is supported by ads served through Unity Technologies' advertising services (Unity LevelPlay and Unity Ads). To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, these services may collect:

  • Advertising identifiers (such as IDFA) and device identifiers
  • Device information such as model, OS, language and connection type
  • Approximate location (country or region, estimated from IP address)
  • Ad impressions and interactions

On iPhone, iOS asks for your permission before any tracking across other companies' apps and websites. You can play the App the same way if you decline; you will then see non-personalized ads. You can change this at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

In regions where consent is required (such as the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), the App asks for your consent first. US residents can choose "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information". Both can be changed at any time in the App under Settings > Privacy.

For details, see:

  • Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy (Unity Ads, Unity LevelPlay, Unity Gaming Services)

3. Third-party services

Online matches and rankings use Unity Gaming Services by Unity Technologies (Authentication, Multiplayer, Relay, Leaderboards). The information in section 1 is processed on their servers, which may be located outside your country.

4. Saving to Photos

The App saves a share image to Photos only when you choose "Save Image" in the share feature. The App never reads your photos.

5. Sharing

Except as required by law, we do not share or sell your information to anyone other than the services in sections 2 and 3.

6. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

7. Deleting your information

Information stored on your device is removed when you delete the App. To have your ranking name, icon or records deleted, contact us below. The ranking resets on the 1st of every month.

8. Changes

We will post any changes to this policy on this page.
